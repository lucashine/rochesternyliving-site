Three questions I get every week

“What does my budget actually buy?”

Price alone tells you almost nothing here. The same number buys very different houses in different towns, with different lot sizes, different eras of construction, very different property tax bills. The town write-ups get specific about that.

“How long is the commute, really?”

Not the number a map app gives you at 2pm on a Sunday. What it looks like in February, leaving at 7:40, with the routes people actually take.

“Is now a bad time to buy?”

I won’t tell you the market is about to do anything, because I don’t know and neither does anyone else. What I can do is show you what’s currently on the market in your range and what it’s taking to get an offer accepted.
