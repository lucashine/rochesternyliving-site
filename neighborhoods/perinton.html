Perinton & Fairport

The town of Perinton wraps around the village of Fairport, east of Rochester, and the Erie Canal runs straight through the middle of both. Fairport grew up as a canal port, and the water still shapes how the whole place works: the canal path is the spine people use to walk, run, and bike from one end of town to the other.

What housing is here

Fairport village is the older core: blocks of late-1800s and early-1900s houses on smaller lots, with sidewalks and a walk to Main Street and the canal. Outside the village, Perinton is mostly subdivisions built from the 1960s onward, with bigger lots and a mix of colonials, split-levels, and ranches. Townhouse and condo communities are scattered through town, and the southern end opens up toward Victor.

What makes it Perinton

The Main Street lift bridge opened in 1914 and is genuinely strange: a ten-sided steel truss that crosses the canal at a 32-degree angle, with no two angles the same and no square corners on the deck. It was fully rehabilitated and reopened in 2021.

One practical detail people don’t expect: the village runs its own municipal electric utility, Fairport Electric. If you’re comparing houses inside and outside the village line, ask me about the utility bills alongside the taxes.

Property taxes

In New York the tax bill is built from layers: town, county, and school district, plus a village tax if the house is inside a village. Each layer has its own rate, so two houses at the same price can carry very different bills depending on which side of a line they sit on. Before you make an offer, I’ll pull the actual tax bill for that specific house so it never arrives as a surprise.

Getting around

NY 31 and NY 250 are the local spines, and I-490 is a short drive from most of town for the run into downtown Rochester. Everything in the village is walkable or bikeable along the canal.

Parks, trails, and getting outside

The Erie Canalway Trail runs right through the village. Perinton also has a long network of town trails, including the Crescent Trail, and Fairport Canal Days takes over Main Street every summer.

Schools

Perinton is served primarily by the Fairport Central School District, with some addresses at the edges of town in neighboring districts. Verify the district for any specific address before you rely on it.

The honest trade-off

The lift bridge goes up for boats during canal season, and when it does, Main Street stops. If you live on the far side and you’re in a hurry, you’ll learn the workarounds fast. It’s charming in July and less charming when you’re late.
