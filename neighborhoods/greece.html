Greece

Greece is the largest town in Monroe County by population, about five miles northwest of the city, and it has more than eight miles of Lake Ontario shoreline. Between the lake, the bays, and a chain of ponds along the shore, it has more water than most people expect from a big suburban town.

What housing is here

A lot of Greece was built out in the decades after World War II, so the core of town is capes, ranches, and split-levels on regular suburban lots. Newer subdivisions run further west and north, and along the water you’ll find lakeshore homes and cottages. Because the town is so big, the range is wide, and that makes it one of the more flexible searches in the county.

What makes it Greece

Braddock Bay, in the northwest corner of town, is the centerpiece: a town park with a boardwalk and kayak launch, plus the much larger state Fish and Wildlife Management Area around it. Long Pond, Buck Pond, Cranberry Pond, and Round Pond sit just behind the shoreline.

Property taxes

In New York the tax bill is built from layers: town, county, and school district, plus a village tax if the house is inside a village. Each layer has its own rate, so two houses at the same price can carry very different bills depending on which side of a line they sit on. Before you make an offer, I’ll pull the actual tax bill for that specific house so it never arrives as a surprise.

Getting around

Route 390 runs north-south through town, Ridge Road (Route 104) is the main commercial corridor, and the Lake Ontario State Parkway runs along the shore. The city line is right there.

Parks, trails, and getting outside

Braddock Bay, the Lake Ontario State Parkway, the ponds, and Greece Canal Park, a Monroe County park, down on the town’s south side near the Erie Canal.

Schools

Greece is served primarily by the Greece Central School District, with some addresses at the edges of town in neighboring districts. Verify the district for any specific address before you rely on it.

The honest trade-off

Lakeshore flooding. Lake Ontario flooding has damaged shoreline property in Greece in recent years, and the town has been investing in flood resilience because of it. If a house is near the water, check the flood zone and price the insurance before anything else. And Ridge Road is a long, busy retail strip, with the traffic that comes with it.
