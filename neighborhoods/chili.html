Monroe County · Southwest

Chili

First things first: it’s pronounced CHAI-lye, not like the food. Chili is southwest of the city, with the Genesee River along part of its eastern edge. Black Creek splits the town in two: the land north of the creek is suburban, and the land south of it is still rural and agricultural.

What housing is here

North of the creek, it’s subdivisions: ranches, raised ranches, split-levels, and colonials, mostly postwar onward, around Chili Center and North Chili. South of the creek you get farmland, bigger parcels, and houses on acreage. That split means two very different searches in one town, so tell me which half you’re after.

What makes it Chili

Roberts Wesleyan University is in North Chili and puts on concerts and theater that are open to the public. The Frederick Douglass Greater Rochester International Airport sits along the town’s northeast edge with Gates, and one of its runways points right into Chili.

What it costs

Prices here move month to month, so I don’t print a median and let it go stale. Ask, and I’ll send you the current MLS numbers for Chili: what’s sold recently, at what price, how fast, and what your budget actually buys here right now.

Property taxes

In New York the tax bill is built from layers: town, county, and school district, plus a village tax if the house is inside a village. Each layer has its own rate, so two houses at the same price can carry very different bills depending on which side of a line they sit on. Before you make an offer, I’ll pull the actual tax bill for that specific house so it never arrives as a surprise.

Getting around

I-490 and I-390 are both close, and the airport is about as near as it gets anywhere in the county. Chili Avenue (Route 33A) and Paul Road are the local workhorses.

Parks, trails, and getting outside

Black Creek Park is 1,505 acres of mostly undeveloped county land with trails for hiking, horseback riding, fishing, sledding, and cross-country skiing. There are also boat launches on the Genesee River and on Black Creek.

Schools

Chili is split among three districts: Churchville-Chili, Gates Chili, and Wheatland-Chili. Which one a house is in depends on the street, so verify the district for any specific address before you rely on it.

The honest trade-off

Flight paths. Parts of town near the airport hear the planes. Before you fall for a house on the north or east side, stand in the backyard for twenty minutes on a weekday afternoon.
