Canandaigua

Canandaigua is where Rochester ends and the Finger Lakes begin: a small city at the north tip of Canandaigua Lake, the county seat of Ontario County, surrounded by a town of the same name. My brokerage’s office is on Rochester Road here, so this is a place I know on a weekday, not just on a summer weekend.

What housing is here

Inside the city, it’s older homes on walkable streets off Main Street, a lot of them 19th and early 20th century. Out in the town you’ll find newer subdivisions and more land. Around the lake itself, lakefront and lake-access homes are their own market with their own rules, and it’s worth talking through what “lake access” actually means on a given property before you fall in love with one.

What makes it Canandaigua

The City Pier is lined with a row of historic wooden boathouses, and Kershaw Park runs along the lakefront next to it. Sonnenberg Gardens and Mansion is a Victorian estate that became a New York State Historic Park in 2006. CMAC, the big outdoor concert amphitheater, is here too, and Bristol Mountain is a short drive south.

What it costs

Prices here move month to month, so I don’t print a median and let it go stale. Ask, and I’ll send you the current MLS numbers for Canandaigua: what’s sold recently, at what price, how fast, and what your budget actually buys here right now.

Property taxes

In New York the tax bill is built from layers: town, county, and school district, plus a village tax if the house is inside a village. Each layer has its own rate, so two houses at the same price can carry very different bills depending on which side of a line they sit on. If the house is inside the city of Canandaigua rather than the town, there’s a city tax in the mix and the schedule is different, so ask me which one a listing is actually in. Before you make an offer, I’ll pull the actual tax bill for that specific house so it never arrives as a surprise.

Getting around

Route 332 runs north to the Thruway at Exit 44 in Farmington, and from there I-490 heads west into Rochester. Of everything on this page, this is the longest drive to downtown.

Parks, trails, and getting outside

The lake is the point. Kershaw Park’s beach and lakefront path, the pier, boating all summer, and skiing at Bristol in winter.

Schools

Canandaigua is served primarily by the Canandaigua City School District, with some addresses at the edges of town in neighboring districts. Verify the district for any specific address before you rely on it.

The honest trade-off

Distance. If you commute into Rochester every day, you’re signing up for the longest drive on this list. And in summer, the lake brings visitors and traffic to Main Street and Lakeshore Drive.
