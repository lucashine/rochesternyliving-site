Standardized Operating Procedure for prospective homebuyers

New York Real Property Law § 442-h requires real estate brokers to adopt and publish standardized operating procedures covering prospective homebuyers, including whether identification is required, whether an exclusive agreement is required, and whether pre-approval or pre-qualification is required before a showing.

This procedure is adopted and filed by the brokerage, not by an individual agent. To request the current Standardized Operating Procedure for Keller Williams Realty Gateway, contact the office at (585) 713-2055 or lucashine@kw.com and I will send it to you.
